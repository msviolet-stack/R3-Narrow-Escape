CASE BRIEFING

A Narrow Escape

A crash shattered the quiet school evening. “Someone just tried to kill me!” cried Professor Wren.

An attacker had swung a broom at him. Fortunately, he ducked in time and escaped unharmed. The attacker fled, leaving a clear fingerprint behind.

Detective Felix identified three suspects. Each had been at a different location and carried a different object. Only one was responsible for the attack.

Your task: Find out who attacked Professor Wren, which object they carried and where the attack happened. Use the clues to explain your conclusion.

The rules

Each suspect had exactly one object and visited exactly one location during the incident. Each object and location is used once. The attacker's fingerprint was left at the scene during the attack.

Final Accusation

Teacher Case Solution

Auntie May · Broom · Canteen. Professor Wren escaped unharmed.

SuspectObjectLocation
Sir CedricYo-yoBus bay
Uncle JasperPoisoned appleStaff room
Auntie MayBroomCanteen

The fingerprint places Auntie May in the canteen. The note says: “The poisoned apple was in the staff room.” Uncle Jasper cannot be in the canteen (occupied by Auntie May) or bus bay, so he was in the staff room with the apple. Sir Cedric was at the bus bay with the yo-yo. The remaining object, the broom, belongs to Auntie May.

The poisoned apple is a red herring: it was not used in this attack. A suspect's background is not proof of guilt.
